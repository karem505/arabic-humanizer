# Hooks Library — مكتبة الافتتاحيات

> The first 1–3 lines are all that's guaranteed seen before "See more". The hook decides everything.
> أول 2-3 سطور هي كل المضمون اللي الكل يشوفه قبل "See more". الافتتاحية هي اللي بتقرر.

**Billboard test:** if your hook doesn't read in one glance, rewrite it. Simple enough to fit on a billboard.

---

## The 4 hook archetypes that consistently work (from a 200-post viral analysis)

### 1. Confession — الاعتراف
> "I was doing this wrong for years."
> "كنت فاكر إني فاهم السوق. الـ3 شهور اللي فاتوا أثبتوا العكس."

Requirements: must carry a real cost. Fake confessions ("I used to struggle…") are transparent.

### 2. Counterintuitive — التباين العكسي
> "Everything you know about LinkedIn growth is wrong."
> "المنشور الضعيف جاب عملاء. والفايرل لأ."
> "Most networking events are a waste of time."

### 3. Specific number — الرقم المحدد
> "I posted 47 times. Here's what actually worked."
> "Adam has been out of work for 329 days."
> "تلات سنين وأنا أحاول. النتيجة اتلخصت في حاجة واحدة."

Numbers work when they're *precise and human*, not rounded ("100+ clients" ✗ / "37 clients" ✓).

### 4. Identity challenge — التحدي
> "If you think you understand X, read this."
> "This might upset some people — and honestly, I hope it does."

## More proven patterns — أنماط إضافية مجرّبة

| Pattern | Template | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Receipts-first | "2.5M impressions. Here's what actually worked:" | Numbers as proof for a cause, not bragging |
| Honest desperation | "I'm desperate for work." | Extreme candor — only works if real |
| Public correction | "My LinkedIn says I'm a successful X. Here's what it won't tell you:" | Gap between image and reality |
| Unpopular opinion | "Unpopular opinion: [real opinion]" | Must be genuinely debatable |
| The contrast pair | "الأسبوع اللي فات: فايرل مع صفر عميل. النهاردة: بوست صامت جاب 3." | Before/after tension |
| Mid-action scene | "امبارح كنت في اجتماع… ولاحظت حاجة غريبة." | Drop the reader inside a moment |
| The dumb thing I did | "أول مرة عملت deploy في prod، كسرت staging." | Mistakes are impossible to fake |

## Hook killers — افتتاحيات مقتولة (never use)

| Killer | Why |
|---|---|
| "هل تساءلت يوماً…؟" / "Have you ever wondered…?" | Rhetorical opener template |
| "في عالمنا الرقمي…" / "In today's digital age…" | Empty-opener cliché |
| "السر اللي محدش عارف يقوله" | Promise opening |
| "Here's what nobody tells you" | Faux-insight (-4.3% reach family) |
| "The result?" cliffhangers | -4.8% measured |
| "Stop X. Start Y." | -6.7% measured — worst measured pattern |
| Announcements ("We're thrilled to announce…") | Press-release voice |
| Context-setting warm-ups ("Before I dive in…") | The reader is already gone |

---

## The 2-line rule — قاعدة السطرين

The first 2 lines must create **tension, a question, or a claim to resolve**. If they only set context, the post is dead on arrival.

Test your hook: read only the first 2 lines. Does a stranger *need* to know what comes next?
- If yes → ship it.
- If "it's fine" → rewrite.

اكتب أول سطرين واقراهم لوحدهم. لو حد غريب حاسس إنه *لازم* يعرف الباقي → تمام. لو لأ → أعد الكتابة.
