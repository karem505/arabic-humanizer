# Banned Phrases — قائمة العبارات الممنوعة

> Every phrase here is a documented "AI tell": it appears far more often in AI-generated text than in human writing, and several are *measured* reach-killers on social platforms.
> كل عبارة هنا "علامة AI" موثقة — بتظهر في النصوص المولّدة أكتر بكتير من الكتابة البشرية، وبعضها مُقاس بالأرقام كقاتل وصول.

**Rule of use:** No single word convicts. **Density + emptiness of substance** is the fingerprint. One "however" is fine; a chain of them around hollow claims is the tell.

## Arabic — Structural tells (بنى ممنوعة)

- **Register:** فصحى رسمية مصقولة ومنفصلة طول النص — بدون أي كلمة عامية. (الكتابة الطبيعية بتخلط.)
- **Translated feel ("ركيك/مترجم"):** ترجمة حرفية لشعارات إنجليزية بدل قولها بالطريقة اللي الناس بتتكلم بيها.
- **Sentence metronome:** كل جملة 15-20 كلمة بنفس الإيقاع (stdev أقل من 5 كلمات = مش طبيعي).
- **Symmetrical paragraphs:** كل فقرة 3-4 جمل بالظبط. كسّرها: فقرة من جملة واحدة، والتانية أطول.
- **Rule of three:** «واضح وموجز ومقنع» — تلاتة تلاتة في كل مكان. واحدة في النص كفاية.
- **Zero specifics:** مفيش رقم، اسم، أو مثال حقيقي — أضعف نقطة على الإطلاق (**-23% وصول**).
- **Neutrality bias:** مفيش رأي حاد — كل وجهة نظر "ليها ما يبررها". البشر مش كده.
- **Excessive punctuation:** شرطات طويلة وترقيم مبالغ فيه.

## English — Hard ban list (kill on sight)

| Phrase | Why it reads AI | Human alternative |
|---|---|---|
| delve / dive into | #1 AI verb (25× pre-ChatGPT rate) | "look at" / just say the thing |
| tapestry · testament · realm · myriad · plethora | AI vocabulary cluster | say it plainly |
| underscore (v.) · showcase · highlight (v.) | AI verbs | "shows" / "this matters because…" |
| pivotal · crucial · vital · paramount | Hollow big adjectives | name the consequence |
| robust · seamless · comprehensive · holistic · dynamic | Corporate gloss | the concrete fact that made you say it |
| game-changer · synergy · paradigm · ecosystem | Marketing clichés | specific outcome |
| leverage · utilize · facilitate · streamline | Verb inflation | use · help · cut steps |
| moreover / furthermore / additionally (starting a paragraph) | "Dead giveaway" — ~0% of top creators do it | start with the point |
| "it's not X, it's Y" / "not just X, but Y" | Negative parallelism — measured **−4.9% reach** | state it directly |
| "here's the thing" / "here's what nobody tells you" | Faux-insight opener | open mid-action |
| "let that sink in" / "read that again" | Frequency anomaly (28×/22× normal) | trust the reader |
| "the result?" / "stop X, start Y" / "the key is" | Measured **−4.8% / −6.7%** reach | connect cause → result |
| "in today's fast-paced world" / "in the digital age" | Empty-opener cliché | delete |
| "it's worth noting" | 0% of top-100 creators as a habit | delete |
| "studies show" / "experts agree" (uncited) | Fake authority | name the study or cut it |
| "in conclusion" / "ultimately" | Recap ending | end on the newest thing |
| "I hope this helps" / "let me know if…" | Chatbot artifact | delete |

## English — Structural tells

- **Uniform sentence length (low burstiness):** 15-20 words every sentence. Human prose: stdev/mean ≈ 0.6-1.2; model output clusters ≈ 0.2-0.4.
- **One-sentence-per-line stacks:** 91% of analyzed AI LinkedIn posts use the "sentence + blank line" silhouette *throughout* the whole post. (Occasional line breaks for beats = fine; the whole-post pattern = tell.)
- **Trailing "-ing" pseudo-analysis:** "…, highlighting the team's commitment" / "…, underscoring its importance". They never contain information → delete.
- **Copula avoidance:** "serves as / stands as / represents" where "is" belongs.
- **Em-dash drumbeat:** >20 per 1,000 words in short prose. LinkedIn em-dash usage went 2% → 15.6% post-AI; top creators stay near 3%.
- **Uniform paragraphs:** every paragraph exactly 3-4 sentences. Break the symmetry.

## Do NOT strip these (they're human, and some help)

- A genuine closing question (98% of top creators use one).
- Real candor — "let's be honest" followed by something actually honest (**+4.6%**).
- Perfect grammar, mixed registers, clinical+emotional tone — none of these are AI evidence by themselves.
- Transition words in isolation, formal prose *in itself*, unsourced content.

## Detector warning

Don't optimize for "100% human" detector scores. Detectors are unreliable in both directions: 61% false-positive rate on essays by non-native English writers (Stanford study); OpenAI retired its own classifier at 26% detection / 9% false positives. **Fix substance and voice — not detector scores.**
