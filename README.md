## Why this exists — ليه الريبو ده موجود

AI-sounding copy doesn't just irritate readers — it measurably hurts reach and engagement:

- Posts with **no concrete detail and no lived experience lost 23% reach**. One verifiable detail: **+14%**. A real personal moment: **+25%**.
- AI-style posts got **45% less engagement** than human-written posts in a 2,726-post study.
- **81% of long-form LinkedIn posts** were classified as likely AI-written (Jul 2026). LinkedIn now says it can identify "generic" content with **94% accuracy**.
- The worst patterns cost the most: *"It's not X, it's Y"* → **−4.9% reach**; *"Stop X, start Y"* → **−6.7%**; *"Here's how/what…"* openers → **−4.3%**.

**الخلاصة بالعربي:** الكتابة اللي بتبان AI مش بس بتضايق القارئ — بتقلل الوصول والتفاعل. الحل مش إننا "نخبّي البصمة"، الحل إن الكتابة يبقى فيها إنسان: موقف، رأي، وتفصيلة حقيقية. الريبو ده هو الكلش ليست بتاع ده — لقواعد الكتابة بالشكل البشري (human copywriting).

## The 10 core rules — القواعد العشرة

1. **Open with a moment, not an announcement.** A confession, a surprising contrast, a human number, a scene. Never a press release.
2. **One narrator voice.** "I found", "this surprised me", "honestly". Admit uncertainty. Report-like neutrality is the tell.
3. **Break the symmetry.** Shortest sentence 4–6 words; longest 25+. Paragraphs of visibly different sizes. The metronome rhythm is what makes text *feel* machine-written.
4. **Numbers are a point, not a dump.** 1–2 in the caption; the rest belongs in the carousel/chart/appendix.
5. **Hard-banned patterns.** See [banned phrases](docs/banned-phrases.md) — "it's not X, it's Y", "here's how", "the result?", rule-of-three everywhere, em-dash floods.
6. **End on a real point — or a question about experience.** Not "what do you think?". Questions that ask for a lived experience get 4–6× more comments.
7. **At least one verifiable detail per piece.** A version number, a price, a name, a date, or something that actually happened to you.
8. **Egyptian colloquial as base layer + English tech terms stay English.** "الـ GPU", not a translated form. No sudden MSA jumps, no tashkeel, no emojis.
9. **Lists: fewer, and uneven.** Max 4 items, visibly different lengths. Never "**Word:** explanation" bullets.
10. **The caption is not a summary of the carousel.** It's one personal angle on the topic.

|---|---|---|
| delve · tapestry · testament · pivotal | في عالمنا الرقمي · في خضم |
| underscore (v.) · showcase · robust | من الجدير بالذكر · تجدر الإشارة |

## FAQ

**How do I make my Arabic writing sound less like AI?**  
Cut the formal connectors (علاوة على ذلك، من الجدير بالذكر), open with a moment instead of an announcement, vary sentence lengths, and add one verifiable detail. The [style guide](STYLE-GUIDE.md) and [banned list](docs/banned-phrases.md) walk through each fix.

**What phrases does ChatGPT overuse in Arabic?**  
«في عالمنا الرقمي»، «من الجدير بالذكر»، «تجدر الإشارة إلى»، «لا شك أن»، «علاوة على ذلك»، «في الختام»، «يسلط الضوء على»، «مما يعكس»، «نقطة تحول» — full table with alternatives in [`docs/banned-phrases.md`](docs/banned-phrases.md).

**Does this work for English too?**  
Yes. The rhythm, hook, and banned-pattern rules come from analyses of hundreds of viral English LinkedIn posts — plus Arabic-first research for dialect writing.

**Is this a detector-bypass tool?**  
No — and that's deliberate. Detectors are unreliable in both directions (up to 61% false positives on non-native English). This repo fixes **substance and voice**, which is what actually moves reach.

**Can I use it for LinkedIn, marketing copy, newsletters?**  
Yes — it's built for social copy (LinkedIn / Facebook / X), but the rules apply to any short-form writing that should read human.

**هل ينفع للعامية المصرية؟**  
أيوه — الدليل كله مبني على العامية المصرية كلغة أساس مع المصطلحات التقنية بالإنجليزي. وفي قسم كامل عن علامات الكتابة بالذكاء الاصطناعي في العربي في `docs/ai-tells.md`.

**Is it free?**  
Yes. MIT licensed.
